# Homepage assets

Locally hosted assets from the approved ZhuDuo interactive design preview.

The architecture photographs are by Clay Banks on Unsplash. Individual source
URLs and license links are recorded in `sources.json`. They illustrate possible
settings, not delivered ZhuDuo projects or product specifications. Cards without
a CMS image explicitly label their fallback as reference photography.

DM Serif Display (regular and italic) and DM Sans (variable) were obtained from
the official Google Fonts repository. Their SIL Open Font Licenses are included
as `OFL-display.txt` and `OFL-sans.txt`. Fonts and photographs are served locally;
visiting the homepage does not fetch fonts or images from a third-party CDN.

The page is an independent implementation of the approved preview. It does not
include source code, logos, or proprietary assets copied from the reference site.
